# Immagini degli esercizi visivi

Asset pubblicato: `assets/objects/realistic-atlas.webp`, 1200 × 600 px. Un'immagine unica con 6 colonne e 3 righe. Mappa e rendering in `shared/object-images.js` e `shared/object-images.css`.

Le immagini sono state generate con lo strumento integrato ImageGen, poi ottimizzate in WebP per il sito. Sono rappresentazioni fotografiche sintetiche per l'identificazione degli oggetti, non materiali di un test clinico standardizzato.

Ordine delle celle:

1. Mela, occhiali, telefono, chiave, scarpa, sedia.
2. Auto, fiore, gatto, casa, palla, gelato.
3. Palloncino, cane, razzo, pizza, elefante, cella vuota.

Prompt utilizzato:

Create ONE production sprite atlas image for an Italian adult cognitive matching exercise. Photorealistic catalog photography, soft studio light, pure white background, no text, no numbers, no borders, no logos. Exact regular 6 columns x 3 rows, all 18 cells equal square dimensions, overall aspect ratio 2:1. Each requested subject completely within its own cell with generous white margin, centered, recognisable silhouette, consistent scale, no overlaps between cells. Read left to right row by row: ROW 1: a red apple with leaf; realistic black reading eyeglasses; a neutral modern smartphone; a single metal house key; a single brown leather shoe; a simple wooden dining chair. ROW 2: a red compact car; a sunflower with short stem; a sitting domestic cat; a small realistic detached house; a black and white soccer ball; an ice cream cone with scoops. ROW 3: a red balloon with short string; a sitting golden retriever dog; a realistic model space rocket; a whole pizza on a simple white background; a standing realistic elephant in side view; an EMPTY white cell. Every cell contains only its exact subject, no additional objects. Crisp familiar object identification photographs, suitable for elderly patients, detailed natural surfaces, not emoji, not flat illustration, not cartoon. The fixed cell ordering is essential because software will address the grid positions.

Le nuove prove includono `stimulusVersion: realistic-v1` nelle impostazioni salvate: vengono confrontate con prove che usano gli stessi stimoli e impostazioni. I risultati precedenti restano conservati.
